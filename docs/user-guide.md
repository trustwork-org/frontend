# User Guide

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for the most common TrustWork workflows for clients and freelancers.

The job lifecycle on TrustWork follows a structured path from creation to completion, with built-in safeguards at every stage.

## For Clients: Hiring and Management

### Posting a Job
1. Click **Post Job** in the navigation bar.
2. Define the job category, deadline, and total budget.
3. Break the budget into at least one milestone with a description and amount.
4. Confirm the deposit. The 2% platform fee will be calculated automatically.

### Approving an Applicant
1. Browse applications on your job detail page.
2. Review freelancer profiles and their Soulbound Reputation tiers.
3. Click **Approve Applicant** to assign the freelancer. The job status will change to **ACTIVE**.

### Reviewing Work
1. When a freelancer submits a milestone, you will receive a notification.
2. Review the submission (e.g., code repository link or document).
3. Click **Approve Milestone** to release funds or **Reject** to request revisions.

## For Freelancers: Working and Earning

### Applying for Work
1. Browse the **Job Board** and filter by your skills.
2. Click **Apply** on a job that interests you.
3. Ensure your profile is fully set up to increase your chances of selection.

### Submitting Milestones
1. Once a job is **ACTIVE**, navigate to the milestone tracker.
2. Provide a submission note (e.g., a link to the finished work).
3. Click **Submit Milestone**. This freezes the funds for that milestone until the client reviews it.

## Handling Disputes

If a client refuses to approve a submission or a freelancer fails to deliver, either party can **Raise a Dispute**.

1. Navigate to the disputed milestone.
2. Click **Raise Dispute** and provide an IPFS link to your evidence (screenshots, logs, etc.).
3. The Dispute DAO will assign arbitrators to review the case and issue a verdict within 48 hours.
