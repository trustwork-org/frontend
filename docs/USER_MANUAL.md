# User & Operational Manual

This manual provides standard operating procedures for the three primary roles within the TrustWork ecosystem.

## 1. The Client (Hirer)

### Best Practices
- **Milestone Granularity**: Break large projects into 3-5 milestones. This ensures continuous delivery and limits dispute exposure.
- **Verification**: Only click "Approve" after verifying the work. On-chain payments are final.
- **Cancellations**: You can cancel an `OPEN` job for a full refund. For `ACTIVE` jobs, the freelancer must first use the `selfReportPoorWork` function.

## 2. The Freelancer (Talent)

### Best Practices
- **Submission Notes**: Always provide a clear, public link (e.g., GitHub, Figma, Vercel) when submitting a milestone.
- **Reputation**: Your Soulbound Tier is your strongest asset. Avoid flags by maintaining clear communication with the client.
- **Self-Reporting**: If you cannot finish a job, self-report early. It prevents a "Lost Dispute" flag and preserves your account.

## 3. The Arbitrator (Judge)

### Procedures
- **Evidence Review**: Access the IPFS CIDs provided in the Dispute Panel. Compare the job description with the submitted work.
- **Neutrality**: You earn the same fee regardless of the winner. Your only goal is to align with the truth.
- **Deadlines**: You have 48 hours to vote. Inactivity results in a 10% collateral slash.
